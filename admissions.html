Admissions Information

Welcome to the entry gateway of His Grace School. Learn about our entry paths, expectations, available classrooms, and documentation guidelines.

Admission Requirements

Nursery Department

Pupils entering our Nursery, Creche or Playgroup classes must have reached a minimum age of 2 years by September of the entry year. Parents must provide full immunization charts and proof of healthy physical status.

Primary Department

Pupils enrolling for Primary classes must have reached a minimum age of 6 years by September of the entry year. Candidates undergo a friendly oral reading and logical assessment to check classroom placement readiness.

Secondary Department

Candidates seeking enrollment in JSS 1 - JSS 3 or SSS 1 - SSS 2 must have successfully graduated from primary school. A standard, competitive screening exam in English Language, Mathematics, and General Studies is conducted for proper placement.

Admission Procedure

1

Acquisition of Form

Parents/Guardians can obtain our physical admission booklet from the school registration desk on weekdays.

2

Submission & Verification

Complete all questions in the booklet. Submit it at the registrar’s desk together with copies of birth certificates and prior academic grades.

3

Scheduled Screening

Bring the child on the designated Saturday for our interactive math, logical, and basic english literacy evaluation screening.

4

Offer & Fee Settlement

Successful candidates receive official admission letters. Clear registry costs to collect books, badges, and school uniforms.

Required Documents

Please ensure the following credentials are fully compiled and attached upon submitting the application form:

  • Photocopy of candidate's Certified Birth Certificate (NPC endorsed).
  • Two (2) recent colored passport-sized photographs of the student.
  • Previous terminal academic transcripts / last Termly Report Card.
  • Medical fitness clearance form / pupil immunization card copy.
