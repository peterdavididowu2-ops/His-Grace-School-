Easy Enrollment

How to Apply

We have structured a highly transparent and seamless four-step admissions track.

1

Obtain Form

Purchase admission application booklet at the school office, or send an inquiry to download.

2

Submit Credentials

Complete the form and submit alongside birth certificate copies and previous academic transcripts.

3

Screening Exam

Prospective pupils will sit for a friendly assessment exam (maths, logic & literacy).

4

Admission Offer

Successful candidates are offered admission, complete fees registration and purchase uniforms.

Screening Exams

Entrance Examination Details

We schedule friendly evaluation examinations to understand a child's strengths, weaknesses, and academic level to place them correctly.

Exam Days: Every Saturday from July to September.
Duration: 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM.
Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, and General Logical Reasoning.

Required Documents for Screening:

  • Photocopy of child's Birth Certificate (NPC endorsed).
  • Two (2) recent colored passport photographs.
  • Last Term Academic Report Sheet from previous school.
  • Proof of payment of registration form (₦5,000).
