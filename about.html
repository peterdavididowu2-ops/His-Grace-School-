About Our Institution

Discover the legacy, values, and outstanding leadership that set His Grace School apart.

Our History & Vision

His Grace School was founded on the fundamental principle that every child, regardless of social background, deserves standard and modern qualitative instructions. It was established in the peaceful community of Abeokuta, Ogun State, to provide a distraction-free campus layout where pupils can fully focus on their academic pursuits and leadership growth.

Over the years, we have grown into a multi-tiered educational model school encompassing **Nursery, Primary, and Secondary education**. Our core strength remains our personalized attention to student welfare, a highly disciplined academic environment, and a staff body with impeccable credentials.

Our school motto, "Molding Lives for Greatness", guides our daily teaching methods. We do not just teach students to pass exams; we instill the confidence, resilience, and ethical compass they need to excel as global leaders and problem solvers.

To be a premier and globally recognized educational institution that produces highly skilled, self-reliant, disciplined, and morally upright scholars who will excel academically and lead with distinction on the global stage.
