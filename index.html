Our Foundation

About His Grace School

Established with a supreme vision to provide affordable, standard, and highly qualitative education, His Grace School has stood as a beacon of academic excellence and outstanding discipline.

We are situated in the serene and peaceful enclave of Agbugburu Village, Odeda, Abeokuta. Our environment is entirely free from urban noise and distractions, creating the perfect atmosphere for study, deep learning, and character building.

At His Grace, we run an enriched curriculum that integrates local national requirements with creative thinking paradigms. This guarantees our children remain competitive in regional examinations and global standards.

Programs of Study

Academic Levels We Offer

His Grace School caters to children from early discovery stages up to preparatory secondary school graduation.

Ages: 2 - 5 Years

Nursery School

Foundational Discovery & Play

Our nursery program is carefully designed to foster critical thinking, social skills, and cognitive development in young children through integrated play-based and structured learning.

Curriculum Highlights:

Early literacy & sensory development
Creative arts & motor skill play
Phonics & basic numeracy
Ages: 6 - 11 Years

Primary School

Molding Academic Excellence

Focusing on core academic subjects alongside character modeling. Our primary curriculum equips pupils with strong foundational knowledge, digital literacy, and critical values.

Curriculum Highlights:

Mathematics & Science proficiency
Computer education & coding intro
Creative Writing & Moral Instruction
Ages: 12 - 17 Years

Secondary School

Greatness & Global Competitiveness

Preparing future leaders for external certifications (WAEC, NECO, JAMB) and tertiary education. We offer comprehensive science, arts, and commercial classes with strict guidance.

Curriculum Highlights:

Advanced STEM & Business studies
WAEC / NECO / JAMB preparation
Career guidance & leadership workshops
